Section 2

Scope and method

This report documents Northwind Traders's review of access to PCI-scoped data in Microsoft 365 from 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-31. It was prepared to support Northwind Traders's PCI DSS Attestation of Compliance for the assessment period ending 2026-10-31.

Scope. The 3 folder sets whose access approval was in force during the period, and the access Polar Sky records for them: every member, every group kept or dropped, and every direct share kept. This report covers only these folder sets in Microsoft 365. Other systems in Northwind Traders's cardholder data environment are reviewed separately.

How the review was done. Access was reviewed as it changed during the period, not in a single campaign at the end. For each folder set, Polar Sky proposed who should keep access and why, and the data owner or another authorized approver reviewed and approved that access in Polar Sky. Polar Sky made the removals in Microsoft 365 and recorded the result of each one.

Who decided. For people Polar Sky proposed, the decision is the approval of their folder set's access that included them; Attachment A shows anyone proposed after an approval was withdrawn. A person who added someone or changed their access is named, with the date. Each approval is recorded with who approved it and when.

Requirement. PCI DSS v4.0.1 Requirement 7.2.4: all user accounts and related access privileges, including third-party and vendor accounts, are reviewed at least once every six months. The review confirms that access remains appropriate for each person's job function, that inappropriate access is addressed, and that management acknowledges the remaining access is appropriate.
